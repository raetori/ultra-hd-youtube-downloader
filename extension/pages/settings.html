Shortcuts

⌥⇧G download the page you're on  ·  ⌥⇧C open the CosmoGrav menu

Change them, or add one for "Grab all media on this page", in Chrome's shortcut settings →

Updates

The extension comes with the CosmoGrav app. When CosmoGrav updates, it brings the matching extension, and Chrome picks it up on its own within the hour (or right away when you open the CosmoGrav menu).

Privacy

Everything runs on your computer. The extension talks only to the CosmoGrav app, never to a server. No accounts, no tracking, no history kept by the extension. Settings stay in this browser.
